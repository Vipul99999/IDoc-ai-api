# High-Value Product Capabilities

This layer turns the platform into an operating system for real business workflows.

## Structured Extraction

Documents produce reusable data: contact details, dates, amounts, invoice numbers, tax IDs, skills, parties, clauses, and simple tables.

## Readiness and Validation

Each document receives print, privacy, OCR, business automation, and malware readiness checks.

## Human Review

The review queue automatically identifies documents that need privacy approval, print-quality fixes, or extraction verification.

## Integration Readiness

Scoped API keys support print vendors, university portals, enterprise automations, and government integrations.

## Governance

Retention policy APIs provide legal-hold-aware dry runs before deletion.

## Analytics

Admin analytics expose average quality, readiness, high-risk documents, automation readiness, open review tasks, and estimated print revenue potential.
